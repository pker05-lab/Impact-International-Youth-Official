About Impact International Youth

Built for the next generation.

A youth-led platform focused on connection, knowledge, discussion, skills, and meaningful opportunities.


Meet the Team Our Partners
Our purpose

Creating space to learn, connect, and grow.

Impact International Youth was created around a simple idea: people should have opportunities to meet new faces, exchange ideas, stay informed about the world, and prepare themselves for what comes next.

Keep our community informed about important global issues and developments.
Create opportunities for discussion, participation, and collaboration.
Build practical skills in communication, leadership, research, diplomacy, and critical thinking.
Recognize achievement, participation, leadership, and contribution.
Our vision

A connected, informed, and prepared generation.

We want our events to be useful, enjoyable, and open to people who want to learn something new or contribute something meaningful.
