About IIMUN

Where diplomacy meets the next generation.

Impact International Youth MUN is the Model United Nations initiative of Impact International Youth. It gives participants a structured environment to represent countries, discuss international issues, conduct research, negotiate, collaborate, and develop confidence in public speaking.
